# Abhinesh Portfolio — Core Idea

## North Star

# I GO WHERE THE PROBLEM IS.

This is not a conventional designer portfolio. It is a product experience that positions Abhinesh as a **forward-deployed product designer**: someone comfortable entering ambiguous situations, understanding the real problem, prototyping quickly, collaborating with technology, and moving toward a useful MVP or shipped product.

### Positioning

> Forward-deployed product designer turning ambiguity into something real.

> I work across product thinking, UX, visual design, AI and code — getting close to the problem, figuring out what needs to exist, and building the smallest useful thing that can teach us something.

## What the visitor should believe

- He is comfortable with ambiguity.
- He thinks about problems, not just screens.
- He can move from idea to prototype/MVP quickly.
- He understands product, design and technology together.
- He has strong visual craft.
- He is actively experimenting and building.
- His portfolio itself feels like a product.

## Story

```text
AMBIGUITY → CURIOSITY → UNDERSTAND → FRAME → DECIDE → PROTOTYPE → BUILD → TEST → SHIP → LEARN → MOVE FORWARD
```

## Homepage narrative

The story should answer four questions:

1. **Who is he?** — I go where the problem is.
2. **How does he work?** — I don't need a perfect brief.
3. **Can he do it?** — Show real work quickly.
4. **What makes him different?** — Decisions, outcomes, experiments, AI and technical fluency.

## Hero length

Only **3–4 scroll states** before the first major project. This is a portfolio, not an interactive movie intro.

### State 01 — Hook

> **I GO WHERE THE PROBLEM IS.**

`FORWARD-DEPLOYED PRODUCT DESIGNER`

`PRODUCT · UX · AI · BUILD`

### State 02 — Personality

> **I DON'T NEED A PERFECT BRIEF.**

Give me a messy problem, an ambitious idea, or a product that does not exist yet. I'll figure out what needs to be built.

### State 03 — Method

> **FROM AMBIGUITY TO SOMETHING REAL.**

`UNDERSTAND → FRAME → PROTOTYPE → BUILD → TEST → SHIP`

### State 04 — Proof

> **SOME PROBLEMS I'VE BUILT AROUND.**

Immediately reveal the strongest project.

## Visual references

Use the **Symbol Studio** reference for editorial composition, typography, whitespace, thin rules, asymmetric layouts, restrained black/white treatment and project presentation.

Use **Jesko Jets** as inspiration for cinematic sequencing, scroll-driven storytelling, large visual moments, progressive disclosure and purposeful motion.

Do not copy either site's branding, content, imagery, exact layouts or animations.

## Project philosophy

Projects should be introduced through the **problem/question**, not just the project name.

Instead of:

> FundedGlobal — Fintech Dashboard

Prefer:

> **How do you make financial complexity feel trustworthy?**

Then show `FUNDEDGLOBAL`.

## Case-study story

```text
THE SITUATION
↓
THE FRICTION
↓
THE REAL PROBLEM
↓
THE CONSTRAINT
↓
THE DECISION
↓
THE BUILD
↓
THE REALITY CHECK
↓
THE OUTCOME
↓

```

Focus on decisions and consequences, not a dump of design activities.

## Core metaphor

**Forward motion.**

The site communicates:

`problem → movement → transformation → product → next problem`

Animation must support that idea.

## Ending

> **WHAT ARE WE BUILDING NEXT?**

The portfolio should end with an invitation to work on difficult, unfinished or ambitious problems.
